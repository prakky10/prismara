# Prismara: The Last Light - Story Bible and Script

Writing document for the implementer. Every map id, trainer id and creature named here already exists. Anything new (NPCs, flags, scenes) is listed in Section 9.

Placeholders: `{player}` is the player's name, `{rival}` is the companion's name (default Kai). Dialogue lines are written to fit one text-box page (90 characters or fewer). A line with no speaker is narration.

---

## 1. Logline and themes

**Logline.** A year ago the sky prism cracked and the keeper of the last lighthouse vanished. Her child follows her journal across six beacon towns, and learns that the disaster was no accident, no invasion, and no single villain's work. It was seven good people trying to make the light unbreakable, and one of them is still holding the broken night together with her own body.

**Themes.**
- Fear of loss makes people reach for control. Voss chooses stillness, Aurelia chose force. Both wanted "nothing will ever break again."
- Light that is shared freely holds; light that is bound breaks. (The six Trophy virtues: Growth, Resilience, Flow, Courage, Clarity, Remembrance.)
- Guilt versus responsibility. Soren, Lumen, Blaze and Aurelia each carry a piece of that night. Only telling the truth lets them put it down.
- Remembering is not the same as living in the past.

**Tone.** Warm, hopeful, a little melancholy. No violence beyond creature battles. Humor comes from Aunt Wren, Kai's bluster and Edda's dryness.

## 2. Cast

**{player}.** Child of Edda, the Lighthouse Keeper. Silent protagonist (never has spoken lines; reactions are narrated). Want: find their mother. Secret: none, but they are the one who literally carries the journal and the Trophies, so every Keeper sees Edda in them. Arc: from "follow the pages" to deciding what to do with the truth (forgive, rebuild).

**Edda (the player's mother, the Seventh Keeper).** Keeper of the last lighthouse, which relayed the sky prism's light to the six beacons. Dry-humored, practical, stubborn about trust. Want: for the isles' light to stay shared, not owned. Secret: on the night of the fracture she walked into the prism's heart and held the crack closed with her own lantern-light. She did not vanish; she is inside the prism, kept from fading by Voss's twilight. Arc: she is the fixed point the others orbit; her journal is the story's spine; she returns at the Finale and chooses to be a keeper among keepers, not a martyr.

**{rival} (Kai by default).** The player's oldest friend, sharp-tongued and loyal. Want: to bring his older brother Soren home. Secret: Soren has been sending him letters (one bottle, one pin) which Kai has told no one about; he is more afraid that Soren does not want to be found than that Soren is lost. Arc: from "drag him home and win the argument" (battle 1) through anger (2), doubt (3), listening (4), to the altar where he offers his hand instead of a challenge. Ends the game travelling the isles to restore the beacon towns with Soren.

**Soren (Kai's older brother, 17).** Edda's lighthouse apprentice, with a brass lantern pin. Quiet, wry, kind to creatures. Want: to undo what he thinks he did. Secret (three layers): (1) he is the boy who pulled the shutter lever on the night, on the Champion's signal; (2) he joined Voss because Voss alone told him it was not his fault; (3) he has been quietly sabotaging Eclipse and guiding the player (the Whisperwood note, the half-open breaker). Arc: from hiding to holding the lantern at the altar to confessing aloud and standing with the player at the Summit. Ends as the lighthouse's apprentice again.

**Voss (Eclipse leader, former Twilight Keeper).** Calm, courteous, tender, afraid. Edda's closest friend and fellow keeper; held the "dark half" of the Concord. Want: to stop time at the moment before Edda faded. Secret: his twilight is not a weapon; it is a cradle. The Duskmoor altar, with a cold-iron lantern forged at Ashfall, keeps Edda from burning out inside the prism. Eclipse's power drain feeds it. He has told his grunts never to hurt Edda's child. Arc: antagonist whose tragedy is revealed by Raven; defeated, he admits fear has cost the isles more than the fracture; at the Summit he lets her go.

**Aurelia (Champion, Head Keeper).** Regal, warm, precise. Edda's and Voss's old teacher. Want: an unbreakable beacon so that no keeper would ever fail again. Secret: she designed and led the Concord, signalled the lighthouse, and then ordered all keepers to stay silent, and became Champion so she could guard the Summit and the truth. She is also the veiled Pilgrim at Cinder Peak. Arc: perfect host -> hidden penitent -> confession -> gives up the Lens freely and agrees to answer for it publicly. Her battle is a test of whether the player can carry the truth without breaking, then she asks for forgiveness.

**Prof. Lumen (Emberleaf Lab).** Gentle, scatterbrained scholar of the sky prism; gives out starters. Want: to repair the prism. Secret: he wrote the Concord's mathematics; Edda warned him it would fail, and he said he was "nearly sure". He withheld the last pages of her journal out of shame. Arc: dithering helper -> confession at Geode Hollow -> opens the lab's archive to everyone at the end.

**Aunt Wren.** (Replaces "Aunt Mira"; renamed to avoid clashing with trainer Mira.) Edda's sister-in-spirit and the player's guardian. Brisk, warm, funny. Her humor and grief are the home note. She is why the socks line becomes a callback in the epilogue, not a tonal clash.

**The six Keepers.** Each one holds one piece of the night and breaks silence after the player earns their Trophy.
- **Ivy (Verdant, Growth).** Gentle, patient. Piece: Edda planted a green shoot a week before the crack and warned her; the seven keepers were summoned to the Summit; the Summit later ordered silence.
- **Volta (Storm, Resilience).** Blunt, practical. Piece: her logs show a timed pulse from the Summit at 11:58 pm; a boy with a lantern pin quietly left a breaker half open and saved her turbines.
- **Marin (Tide, Flow).** Bold, salty. Piece: she ferried the beacon-glass crates; at dawn she pulled a sobbing boy from the surf (Soren) and handed him to a tall man in dusk-grey (Voss); she carried the journal ashore to Lumen.
- **Blaze (Ember, Courage).** Gruff craftsman. Piece: he forged the Summit ring, knew it was too thin, and stayed silent; later he forged Voss's cold-iron lantern without asking why.
- **Opal (Facet, Clarity).** Cool, exact. Piece: her Record Wall shows the Concord and names who signalled; she carved it despite the order of silence.
- **Raven (Twilight, Remembrance).** Soft-spoken poet; Voss's former student and the Twilight Keeper now. Piece: she knows Edda is inside the prism and that Voss is holding her.

**Ace Nerys.** Route 7 trainer, once Aurelia's pupil. Her one job is to show that something has changed in the Champion.

**Eclipse grunts.** Ordinary people who lost someone in the fracture. They are not cruel; Voss told them to slow the keeper's child, not harm them.

## 3. The mystery: what really happened

### The truth (chronological)

1. **Years before.** Beacons fail one at a time as keepers age or leave. Aurelia, Head Keeper, becomes afraid that the isles' light depends on seven fragile people.
2. **The Concord plan.** Aurelia proposes the Concord: bind the six elemental lights into one permanent flame at Prism Summit. Lumen supplies the mathematics ("nearly sure"). Blaze forges a brass ring ("too thin, but it only has to hold once"). Volta's grid will supply a power pulse. Marin ferries the beacon-glass. Ivy grows the living root. Opal's crystals will record it. Voss holds the twilight counterweight. Edda says it is too much: a shared light must be shared willingly.
3. **A week before.** Edda plants a green shoot in Thistlebrook, warns Ivy, and writes in her journal. All seven keepers receive a summons for midnight.
4. **The night.** At midnight Aurelia signals the lighthouse with her lamp. Soren, Edda's apprentice, pulls the shutter lever. Volta's grid pulses at 11:58. Six lights pour into one ring. The elements, forced together, turn on each other. The prism cracks.
5. **The sacrifice.** Edda steps into the prism's heart with her lantern and holds the crack shut with her own light. The white flash is her; the isles' beacons go dark.
6. **The cradle.** Voss tries to follow and cannot. He realizes the twilight (the "dark half") can cushion what is left of Edda so she does not fade. He pulls Soren from the surf (via Marin's rescue), tells him "it was not you", and orders a cold-iron lantern from Blaze. He builds the altar at Duskmoor. The cold costs the isles power every night, so Eclipse drains the grid and turns trainers away from anything that might disturb the ritual. If the ritual completes at midnight, Edda is frozen forever.
7. **The cover-up.** Aurelia orders every keeper to stay silent, becomes Champion to guard the Summit, and every year goes veiled to the Ancient Stone at Cinder Peak to light a candle.
8. **The solution (Edda's own words).** If the six lights ever share freely, the prism will hold itself, and she can step out. That is what the six Trophies, earned and not taken, make possible.

### Twists and where each is foreshadowed

**Twist A: The fracture was the Keepers' own doing (the Concord), not an invasion or accident.**
- Foreshadowed: Ivy's grey wood and the shoot (Ch2); Volta's "easy current" (Ch4); Marin's silence about the crates (Ch5); Blaze's cold forge "shared one heart" (Ch6); Lumen going white at the journal; Aurelia's intro line "I kept this beacon the night your mother vanished"; journal pages 1-5 ("too much", "cannot be forced", "it only has to hold once").
- Revealed: Opal's Record Wall (Ch7).

**Twist B: Edda is alive inside the prism, and Voss's twilight is what keeps her from fading. Voss is a protector first and a tyrant never.**
- Foreshadowed: the grunt "keep the glass warm" (Ch3); grunt "He talks to the glass like it can hear" (Ch3); orders to spare the player (Ch1, Ch3); Blaze's cold-iron lantern and the man who cried (Ch6); Raven's original Trophy memory line "the cold that Voss mistook for mercy"; the altar "hums like a held breath".
- Revealed: Raven (Ch8).

**Twist C: Soren is the double agent and the lever boy, and he wrote the forest note in Edda's handwriting.**
- Foreshadowed: the note "in your mother's hand" with a small lantern glyph (Ch1); the pin (Ch2); the half-open breaker (Ch3, Ch4); the bottle letter "the lamp was mine to light" (Ch5); Marin's boy in the surf (Ch5); Kai's growing doubts.
- Revealed: Soren confesses at the altar (Ch9).

**Twist D (smaller): The veiled Pilgrim, Aurelia herself.**
- Foreshadowed: the Pilgrim's remarks (Ch6); Nerys saying Aurelia "stands like she's guarding a grave" (Ch7); the Summit broadcast (Ch4).
- Revealed: Aurelia's confession (Finale).

**Twist E (smaller): Lumen kept the last pages of the journal.**
- Foreshadowed: Marin: "he went white as foam" (Ch5). Revealed: Ch7.

## 4. Chapter-by-chapter script

### Conventions
- **Trigger types.** `ENTRY map` = fires the first time the player enters the map while the requirements hold. `TALK npc` = fires when talking to the NPC. `AFTER trainer` = plays after the trainer's normal defeat dialogue (and for rivals `rival-N-defeated`).
- **Auto flag.** Every scene sets `scene-<id>` when finished so it plays once. Only additional flags are listed under "Sets".
- **Requires.** Flags needed. A `!` means the flag must not be set.
- Scenes run in the order listed per chapter when more than one qualifies on the same trigger.
- Speaker labels in parentheses (voice) mean off-screen.
